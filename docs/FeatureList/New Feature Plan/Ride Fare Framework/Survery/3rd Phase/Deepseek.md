- **Point estimate:** 43 BDT/m³
- **Low–high range:** 38–43 BDT/m³

- **Basis:** BERC order sets CNG consumer gas price at Tk 38/m³. Station owner commission brings pump price to Tk 43/m³. Petrobangla has proposed raising CNG to ~Tk 65/m³, but this remains a proposal as of August 2026.
