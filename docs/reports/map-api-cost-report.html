Later phases (not modeled) — food, shop, parcel
Food delivery, shop logistics and parcels are excluded from every headline number on this page. They were part of the previous competitor-scale simulator and are intentionally out of scope for the pilot-economics view. They return as modeled phases only after the rides pilot.
